# Free-Stuff-Near-You · Logo Concepts v2

Status: **unadopted refinements for review**. This round continues the user's selected **Open Value** direction from `v1/03-open-value.svg`, preserving its rounded, right-facing price-tag idea. The v1 artwork is unchanged. Each direction now includes a dollar sign and a zero, keeping **$0** explicit rather than implying a paid product. All artwork is original, authored from editable SVG geometry.

The repository still has no product implementation or approved identity. The same working assumption remains: a future app for discovering nearby free items and supporting community reuse. The exact display name is **Free-Stuff-Near-You**.

| Direction | Refinement | Exact palette | Likely use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Sage Cutout | Direct continuation of the chosen v1 Open Value silhouette. A broad $0 and tag hole are transparent cutouts, preserving the free-value idea while introducing the dollar sign. | #6FA889; #214E3B | Compact app mark, listing badge, and primary header identity. |
| 02 · Mint Tilt | The same Open Value tag is tilted twelve degrees. A light mint face, subtle sage outline, and deep-green $0 create a welcoming printed-ticket character. | #C6E7D1; #6FA889 | Community avatar, app mark, and informal brand accents. |
| 03 · Gentle Ticket | Open Value becomes a generous capsule with the original right-facing tag point retained. Broad geometry supports a friendly $0 without losing the price-tag association. | #A4C9B1; #214E3B | App mark, offer badge, and spacious header lockup. |

The shared deep-green wordmark is `#214E3B`. Mint Tilt also uses this color for its $0. Sage Cutout uses true transparent $0 and tag-hole cutouts; the other marks use deep-green $0 and transparent tag holes. Gentle Ticket moves the price lettering slightly right to sit comfortably in the rounder left end. The monochrome samples retain these geometries, reversing the printed $0 to white where appropriate.

Each direction has one horizontal lockup and one companion mark-only file:

- `01-sage-cutout.svg` and `01-sage-cutout-mark.svg`
- `02-mint-tilt.svg` and `02-mint-tilt-mark.svg`
- `03-gentle-ticket.svg` and `03-gentle-ticket-mark.svg`

The six individual logo files have transparent surrounding canvases, accessible titles and descriptions, live editable text, and vector paths. Transparent openings use editable luminance masks. Compact samples on the sheet communicate the intended 32 px and 64 px use; they are design examples, not completed browser or device checks.

`00-concept-sheet.svg` is the editable flat 1600 × 1000 comparison sheet with three equal columns. `00-concept-sheet.png` is its companion raster export for preview and sharing. The sheet uses paper `#F1F5EF`, white cards `#FFFFFF`, secondary text `#5B7063`, and dividers `#D6E3D9`. These are review deliverables, not product mockups. The PNG export is part of producing the concept artwork, not an application build or UI check.

Wordmarks remain live text, with font stack `Avenir Next, Trebuchet MS, Arial, sans-serif`, weight 700. Font availability may change spacing. No font binary is bundled. Before adopting a direction, choose and license the final font, refine kerning, and outline the approved wordmark where fixed reproduction is required. Companion mark-only files are available for constrained spaces.

No previous rounds, application assets, or implementation files were changed. No identity has been adopted. These concepts have not been tested, built, committed, or pushed.
