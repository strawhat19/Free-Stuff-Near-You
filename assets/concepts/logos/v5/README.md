# Free-Stuff-Near-You · Logo Concepts v5

Status: **unadopted refinements for review**. This round continues the chosen Open Value price-tag identity and the sage, mint-tilt, rounded-ticket, FSNY, and plain treatments. All v1–v4 artwork remains unchanged. The six options retain the same three-column by two-row comparison layout.

The bottom point is now a **short exact geometric twin of the right tag point**, replacing v4's elongated lower endpoint. Both belong to one continuous exterior contour, share the same soft-green fill, and have no narrow connector or separate base.

The geometry is authored by reflection across **x = y** in the 160 × 160 coordinate space. The right-point contour segments and bottom-point segments have identical projections, widths, and curve rounding under that transformation. In particular, the right rounded tip `L135 59 Q140 64 135 69` reflects exactly to the bottom rounded tip `L59 135 Q64 140 69 135` when traversed in the opposite direction. The openings have equal radius **6**, at **(112, 64)** and **(64, 112)** respectively, so their offsets and spacing are identical too. The fuller upper-left corner in Rounded $0 Local stays symmetric across the same axis; it does not change either point motif. The mint variants rotate the entire mark eight degrees, preserving the equality.

The dollar and zero are separate editable **Arial Bold** text glyphs. The dollar is **27 units**, the zero **42 units**, both on baseline **80**. The dollar uses a slightly different muted complementary green; the zero remains deep green `#214E3B`. No uneven custom dollar strokes are reused. FSNY remains a 22-unit Arial Bold initial treatment.

The product assumption remains a future local free-item discovery and community reuse app. No implementation or approved identity is implied. The exact full name **Free-Stuff-Near-You** appears in all six lockups.
