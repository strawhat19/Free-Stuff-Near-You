# Free-Stuff-Near-You · Logo Concepts v6

Status: **unadopted refinements for review**. This round follows the user's selected **soft-rounded local tag**, now tilted consistently, while preserving the matched right/bottom geometry from v5. All v1–v5 files remain unchanged. The user's price, FSNY, and plain choices remain available in **all six soft-green palettes**, with three matching comparison sheets and 18 mark / 18 lockup source pairs.

## One composed dollar-zero lockup

The `$0` is now a **single live SVG text element with adjacent tspans**, rather than two separately positioned characters. Arial Bold is shared across both glyphs. The dollar is 32 units and the zero 42 units, with one shared baseline at 80, text-anchor middle, a −0.4-unit letter spacing, and a −1.4-unit zero kerning adjustment. The compact combined text is optically placed at x=62 inside the main body. The muted dollar green differs subtly from the deep-green zero, preserving the smaller-dollar preference while keeping them one typographic unit.

The **entire tag and its internal type share the same `rotate(-8 80 80)` group**. This applies to $0 and FSNY alike. Characters are not held horizontally inside a tilted tag. The full-name wordmark stays horizontal as a separate companion lockup.

## One selected soft-rounded shape

All palettes use the same continuous soft-rounded outline, not six different logo directions. The right and bottom points remain exact reflections in x=y. Both circular openings have radius 6 at (112, 64) and (64, 112), preserving identical size, rounding, projection, and spacing. There is no long tail, thin neck, separate pin, extra pointer fill, or join. The same single-fill silhouette is used for price, FSNY, and plain alternatives.

These are six palette variants of the user's chosen direction. Colors continue the prior rounds rather than introducing a new identity. The product assumption remains a future local free-item discovery and community reuse app. No implemented functionality or final brand adoption is implied. Every lockup uses the exact name **Free-Stuff-Near-You**.

## Editable artwork and fonts

The 36 individual logo SVGs have transparent surrounding canvases, accessible titles and descriptions, useful viewBoxes, and one filled contour with true transparent even-odd hole cutouts. Live $0 and FSNY text use `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, weight 700. FSNY is 22 units with 0.3-unit tracking. Wordmarks use `Avenir Next, Trebuchet MS, Arial, sans-serif`, weight 700. No font binaries are bundled. Font fallback can alter spacing; before adopting a final identity, choose and license the final typeface and outline the approved text when fixed reproduction is required. Current files preserve editability for review.
