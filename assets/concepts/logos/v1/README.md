# Free-Stuff-Near-You · Logo Concepts v1

Status: **unadopted concepts for review**. This is the first logo round; no existing identity is being continued. The SVG artwork is original and authored from geometric paths, not traced from a stock icon, generated image, or third-party logo.

The repository currently contains instruction scaffolding, with no product specification or existing brand assets. These directions assume a future app for discovering nearby free items and supporting community reuse. No trading, payment, location, or user-account feature is implied to exist already. The exact display name remains **Free-Stuff-Near-You**.

| Direction | Idea and character | Exact palette | Likely use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Here to Give | A single destination silhouette with a gift ribbon inside: useful things nearby, with a welcoming tone. The strong pin outline carries the identity while the inset gift adds meaning at larger sizes. | Emerald `#16784F`; forest wordmark `#173D2D`; white insets `#FFFFFF`. | App icon, map discovery header, and community avatar. |
| 02 · Passing It On | Two broad directional ribbons form an open loop. The silhouette conveys circulation rather than ownership; the wide shapes and central breathing space suit compact display. | Forest `#173D2D`; lime `#B9D85B`. | App icon, avatar, reuse campaign, and header. |
| 03 · Open Value | A softly rounded price tag with a zero cut into it. A warm, optimistic route that foregrounds free access and the value of giving things a second home. | Amber `#E8A52A`; forest wordmark `#173D2D`; transparent cutouts. | App icon, listing badge, and header. |

Each direction has a horizontal lockup and a companion mark-only file:

- `01-here-to-give.svg` and `01-here-to-give-mark.svg`
- `02-passing-it-on.svg` and `02-passing-it-on-mark.svg`
- `03-open-value.svg` and `03-open-value-mark.svg`

All six individual logo SVGs have a transparent surrounding canvas, accessible title and description, and editable paths. The small-scale samples on the comparison sheet are design examples, not a completed device or browser validation. Here to Give uses white insets deliberately; Open Value uses true transparent cutouts. A monochrome Passing It On uses the same fill on both ribbons, retaining the gap between them.

`00-concept-sheet.svg` is a flat 1600 × 1000 comparison sheet with three equal columns, primary color lockups, monochrome compact marks, and restrained palette notes. Its paper background is `#F4F3EB`, card fill `#FFFFFF`, secondary text `#5D6D62`, and divider `#D9DFD6`. It is a review sheet, not a product mockup or adopted application asset.

`00-concept-sheet.png` is the companion raster export for convenient preview and sharing; the SVG is the editable source. Exporting the sheet is part of the design deliverable, not an application build or UI check.

Wordmarks remain live editable text. Font stack: `Avenir Next, Trebuchet MS, Arial, sans-serif`, bold weight 700. Font availability changes spacing across platforms; no font binary is bundled or licensed through this concept round. Before final adoption, choose and license the desired font, refine kerning, and outline the approved wordmark if fixed cross-platform reproduction is needed. The long project name is kept in full in the lockups; use the companion mark when space is limited.

No application assets, implementation, or remote repository content have been changed. These concepts have not been committed, pushed, or adopted.
